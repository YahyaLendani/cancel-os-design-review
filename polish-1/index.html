Design review: polish 1

A small slice on the website's Subscriptions page, on top of the refine-2 layout accepted at daf2e7b. The layout, palette and identity are unchanged. Android is unchanged in this slice and is not shown.

  • Edit works on the first press, even before the page is interactive.
  • Focus: it moves into the editor's first field when Edit opens it, and returns to the row's Edit on Done.
  • Placeholders are readable (contrast was 2.57:1, now 5.9:1, in light).
  • The spending bars no longer animate their height.
  • Every website transition uses the shared duration tokens.

Every screen uses synthetic test data (account wizard@example.test, services named "Synthetic …") on a local stand-in server, in headless Chrome 155. No real person's data appears.

Still pending: motion approval, screen readers, physical devices and the release build. Browsers other than Chrome and performance on real hardware were not tested.

Recording: 8c30c11, 13.07 s at real speed

Focus: Edit, then Done (keyboard)

Fixed in 49b86deAt 390 px the returned focus was hidden under the top bar. The historical capture above keeps the defect on record.

  • On 8c30c11's code, after Done, focus was on the row's Edit, but Edit sat at 0–36 px, entirely under the 64 px sticky glass top bar. A point at its centre landed on the bar (WCAG 2.2, 2.4.11, focus not obscured).
  • In 49b86de, after focusing, a small helper scrolls the least distance that clears the mobile top bar and bottom tab bar. It measures them where they are at that moment; nothing moves when the control is already clear, and the scroll is instant, not animated. It is used for Done → Edit and Edit → Name.
  • A check now fails if the focused control is covered, not only if it lacks focus. It passed at 390 px, 1280 px and 200% zoom, for the first and the last row of a longer list, and at 390 px with reduced motion. Before the fix it failed at 390 px for the last row.

Placeholders: 2.57 → 5.9:1 light, 3.35 → 6.64:1 dark

The placeholders "Gym membership" and "9.99" are still lighter than entered values such as "KWD", but less so than before. Distinctness was not tested with people.

Not verified or unresolved

  • PendingMotion approval. The duration changes are a few tens of milliseconds; the recording shows them.
  • PendingScreen readers, on the website and with TalkBack. Focus movement was checked; what a screen reader announces was not observed.
  • PendingPhysical devices and the release build.
  • Not runBrowsers other than Chrome; performance on real hardware.
  • UnresolvedReact error #412. A press made the moment the row appears logs minified React error #412, for Edit and equally for the existing "How to cancel" link. It is reproduced and pre-existing. Its cause and production impact are not established.
  • LimitationThe focus correction handles the marked navigation bars only: the mobile top bar and bottom tab bar. It is not a general solution for every overlapping element; an open menu, a dialog or any other layer over a focused control is not considered.
  • UnresolvedWhether the browser error-reporting SDK records full URLs, including ?edit=<row id>. Not verified. Product analytics and security reports drop the query string.
